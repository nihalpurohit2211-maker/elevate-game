# ✈️ Flappy Plane — City Skyscraper Flight

A simple, lightweight, and addictive Flappy Bird style web game featuring skyscrapers instead of pipes and an airplane instead of a bird.

## 🌟 Features
- **✈️ Plane Flight Dynamics**: Tap or press Space to climb; smoothly pitches up and down with spinning propeller and engine smoke trails.
- **🏙️ Modern Skyscrapers**: Navigate between illuminated city skyscrapers with rooftop aviation warning beacons.
- **💨 Dynamic Winds**: Gentle crosswinds (5–10 seconds per breeze) with visual wind streaks and live HUD indicator.
- **⚡ Close-Call Near Misses**: Skim past skyscraper edges for a **+2 Pts** bonus and sparks.
- **⭐ Collectibles & 🛡️ Shields**: Floating Gold Stars give **+3 Pts**; rare Shield Bubbles absorb **1 crash**.
- **🌅 Day ➔ Sunset ➔ Night Transitions**: The sky and skyline evolve dynamically as your score climbs.
- **📏 Supersonic Stretch**: Plane length grows by **+12%** every 10 points for progressive challenge.
- **👑 Ghost Record Marker**: Marks your personal best in the sky with celebratory fanfare upon beating it.
- **🏆 Persistent Leaderboard**: Hall of Fame rankings saved in `localStorage`.
- **💻 Responsive Design**: Full-width widescreen on laptops, and touch-optimized on Android mobile devices.

## 🕹️ Controls
- **Climb / Flap**: `SPACE`, `W`, `↑`, or **Click / Tap** anywhere.
- **Sound**: Click 🔊 button in the top bar to mute/unmute.
- **Pilot Name**: Click your callsign badge (`✎`) in the top bar to change your pilot name.

## 🚀 Running Locally
Open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or serve with Node:
```bash
node server.js
```
Then visit `http://localhost:3000`.
